1. Using CURIO

CURIO is an independently operated, free educational quiz project offered as a public beta. It provides practice questions, explanations and a country-finding map. No paid subscription, recognized qualification or certified assessment is offered. Contact curiolearn.help@gmail.com with questions.

2. Accounts

Learning content can be used without an account. If you register, use an email address you control, verify it, and protect your password and inbox. Use the site without an account if you are not eligible to consent to account data processing where you live; seek help from a parent or guardian before registering when appropriate. Do not impersonate another person or share access to someone else's account.

3. Learning content

Questions and explanations are for informal learning. Some topics are simplified, debated or subject to correction. Content may contain errors and does not replace professional advice. Please report an issue with the quiz title, question number and a reliable source. A displayed score is practice feedback, not an accredited result.

4. Responsible use

Do not attempt to access another person's account or data, disrupt the service, send automated abuse, misuse email delivery, or exploit a vulnerability against other users. Report security issues privately using the support email or the repository's private reporting channel. Test only accounts and data you control.

5. Progress and availability

Quiz and map results are held in page memory and reset when you refresh or leave. Creating an account does not currently save learning progress. The beta can change or be unavailable during development; uninterrupted service or preservation of temporary results is not promised.

6. Content and third-party material

CURIO branding, code and learning material may be subject to intellectual-property rights. A public repository alone does not grant an open-source license. Natural Earth map data is public domain; other third-party material remains subject to its own terms. External links are provided for reference and lead to services with their own policies.

7. Privacy and account requests

Read the Privacy Policy for account processing, browser storage and service providers. Request account deletion or correction by emailing support from the registered address; account ownership may need to be verified. Do not include passwords or verification codes in your message.

8. Limits and changes

CURIO does not promise that every answer is complete or error-free, that the beta will always be available, or that using it will produce a particular learning outcome.
