1. Introduction and acceptance

These draft Terms of Service describe the proposed rules for using CURIO, an English-language educational quiz website operated in development by an individual owner. They are provided for local testing and review. Before public launch, the owner must finalize these terms and present them to users in a legally appropriate manner. Once finalized and validly presented, using the service signifies acceptance to the extent recognized by applicable law. If you do not agree, you should stop using the service. Mandatory legal rights remain unaffected.

2. Description of CURIO

CURIO offers free educational quizzes, answer explanations, topic collections, and a Daily Six quiz. Scores support informal learning and personal exploration; they are not academic qualifications or professional assessments. Some topics are marked Coming Soon. The current build has no paid subscriptions; Supabase email/password authentication is integrated for local testing.

3. Eligibility and future user accounts

Use must be lawful where you live. No numeric minimum age has been finalized. Children and guardians should read the children’s privacy section before any public use.
