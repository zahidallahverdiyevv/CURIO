Privacy Policy

How CURIO handles account information, browser storage and learning activity.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

CURIO public beta
A free educational project. Accounts are optional; quiz and map results are not saved to an account.

1. About CURIO

CURIO is an independently operated educational quiz project. This notice describes the current public beta. Contact CURIO Support at curiolearn.help@gmail.com about your account, this notice or a privacy request.

2. Account information

Creating an account sends your name, email address and password directly to Supabase Authentication. Your name is account metadata and can be edited on your profile. CURIO does not store a separate copy of your password. Signing in, requesting verification and requesting password recovery also contact Supabase.

3. Quiz and map activity

You can use the learning content without an account. Selected answers, quiz scores and map results stay in the running page's memory. They are not uploaded to an account or saved as a learning history. Refreshing or leaving the page resets this activity.

4. Browser storage

The Supabase SDK stores authentication access and refresh tokens in localStorage to keep you signed in, and a PKCE verifier for confirmation and recovery links. These are functional account storage, not advertising identifiers. Sign out from your profile on a shared device. Signing out does not delete your account, and previously issued access tokens can remain valid until expiry.

5. Services that receive requests

GitHub Pages hosts the published website. Supabase processes authentication and account metadata. Google-hosted fonts and images receive browser resource requests, and Gmail is the configured SMTP provider for account emails. Resource and account requests can include IP addresses and browser headers. The published site serves its JavaScript and compiled styles locally; it does not load an analytics or advertising service. Provider logs and processing are governed by their own policies: GitHub, Supabase and Google.

6. Retention and account deletion

Quiz activity is temporary. Account information remains in Supabase while the account exists. To request account deletion or a copy/correction of account information, email support from the registered address. We may ask you to verify account ownership before acting. Signing out or clearing browser storage alone does not delete the account. Provider logs, delivery records and backups may have separate retention periods under the provider's policies.

7. Privacy requests

Contact support to ask about access, correction, deletion or other privacy rights available where you live. Do not send your password, verification codes or unnecessary identity documents. We will explain the steps needed to verify and handle your request. This notice does not limit rights provided by applicable law.

8. Younger learners

Quizzes can be explored without registering. If you are not old enough to consent to account data processing where you live, use the site without an account and ask a parent or guardian before registering. A parent or guardian can contact support about an account created with a child's information.

9. Security and changes

Authentication requests use Supabase over HTTPS. No secret or service_role key is provided to the browser. Protect your email inbox and use a password you do not reuse elsewhere. No online service can promise absolute security. The date above identifies this version of the notice; material changes to how the service handles data will be reflected here.

10. Contact

Contact curiolearn.help@gmail.com for account and privacy requests. For a security report, avoid posting exploit details or personal data publicly. Read the Terms of Service for the rules of using CURIO.
