1. Introduction

This development-stage Privacy Policy describes the CURIO frontend and local server as inspected on 2 November 2026. CURIO is a free English-language educational quiz platform for an international audience, currently developed and tested locally by an individual owner. This notice must be reviewed and completed before public launch.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: Confirm the individual operator/data controller’s legal identity, location, required contact details, and any required representative or privacy officer.

2. Scope of this policy

This draft covers CURIO pages, quizzes, and the supplied local development server. It describes code-level behavior, not a verified public hosting environment or a complete audit of external providers. Provider websites and services have their own privacy policies. A local page can still make internet requests for external resources. New functionality or public hosting requires a fresh data-flow review.

3. Information you provide

Registration sends your full name, email address, and password directly to Supabase Authentication. Login sends your email and password; recovery and verification requests send your email. CURIO does not manually store passwords. Supabase processes credentials and account metadata. Quiz answers remain separate in page memory.

4. Information collected automatically

A browser loading CURIO sends requests to the local server and, for externally hosted resources, to the relevant provider. Those requests can disclose an IP address, requested resource, browser or device headers, and referrer information depending on browser settings and provider behavior. The supplied local server reads request URLs to serve files; no persistent request logging, visitor database, or analytics integration was found. Third-party or future hosting logs have not been audited. The word analytics in some quiz scripts is an icon name, not a tracking integration.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: Before launch, verify hosting/CDN logs, network metadata, external script behavior, and all resulting personal-data processing.

5. Accounts and authentication

CURIO integrates Supabase email/password registration, login, verification, recovery, and logout. Availability depends on project configuration and email delivery. Verification and recovery links use PKCE and should be opened in the same browser that requested them. Google Sign-In code is prepared but disabled pending OAuth configuration; no Google sign-in request is started while disabled.

6. Quiz progress and learning results

Quiz scripts calculate selected answers, progress, score, and accuracy in the running page’s memory. They do not currently send these results to a backend or save them in localStorage, sessionStorage, or an account. Reloading or restarting the page resets its quiz state. No cloud synchronization, learning profile, or automated high-impact decision system is implemented. Browser history and caching are controlled separately by the browser.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: If saved progress is introduced, define the exact records, storage location, access permissions, export/deletion process, and retention before enabling it.

7. Purposes of processing

The current code uses quiz selections to display answers, explanations, progress, and results during a session. Technical resource requests enable page delivery, typography, styling, and images. There is no implemented advertising, personalized marketing, or visitor analytics system in the supplied code. Account data is used to register, verify and authenticate users, maintain sessions, and recover access. Saved quiz progress is not implemented. Processing must not be expanded silently beyond the published notice.

8. Legal bases where applicable

Which privacy laws and lawful bases apply depends on the operator, audience, processing, and launch locations. No definitive legal basis is asserted in this draft. Consent, contract-related necessity, legal obligation, or legitimate interests may be relevant only if their specific legal requirements are met; none should be assumed to authorize all processing. Accepting terms or reading this policy is not blanket privacy consent.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: For each actual purpose, determine the applicable law and legal basis, identify any legitimate interest and assessment where relevant, and specify required consent/withdrawal controls before launch.

9. Cookies and local storage

The Supabase SDK persists authentication access and refresh tokens in browser localStorage and stores a PKCE verifier for redirect flows. Tokens can keep you signed in across reloads and are refreshed automatically. Sign Out requests Supabase session revocation and clears the local session on success; existing access tokens can remain valid until expiry. Use Sign Out on shared devices. Quiz progress is not persisted. The local server does not set cookies. External resources may have separate storage behavior.

10. Third-party service providers

Supabase provides authentication for project jjrgizykmkzfdbyzzpzd.supabase.co and receives authentication data and technical request metadata. The official Supabase SDK is loaded from cdn.jsdelivr.net. Existing Google Fonts, Tailwind CDN and Google-hosted images also receive resource requests. See Supabase Privacy Policy and Google Privacy Policy. Provider contracts, regions and retention must be confirmed before launch.

11. Data sharing

The inspected quiz code has no feature that sells, publishes, or sends quiz answers to a server. Browser resource requests nevertheless disclose technical information to the external hosts described above. Authentication details are shared with Supabase to operate account access; quiz answers are not sent to Supabase by this implementation. If a future service requires providers, a legal disclosure, or another recipient, identify the categories, purpose, and applicable safeguards before the processing starts. Do not interpret this draft as a guarantee about external providers’ independent activities.

12. International data transfers

Requests to external resource hosts may be routed or processed outside the user’s country. Processing locations and applicable transfer arrangements have not been confirmed. Local development does not eliminate cross-border requests when remote fonts, scripts, or images are loaded.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: Identify actual processing countries and international-transfer requirements. Document applicable adequacy decisions, contractual safeguards or other lawful arrangements, and how users can obtain relevant information, where required.

13. Data retention

Quiz state is temporary. Authentication accounts and metadata are retained in Supabase until removed under the operator’s deletion process. Logout does not delete the account or provider logs. No fixed retention period for accounts, email delivery records, logs or backups has been established; the operator must define these before public launch.

14. Security measures

Authentication uses the official Supabase JavaScript SDK over HTTPS with a frontend-safe publishable key. No secret or service_role key is included in browser code. The SDK manages session tokens; passwords are not stored manually by CURIO. The local server binds to 127.0.0.1. This build has not undergone a production security audit. Third-party scripts execute in the page and require review before launch.

15. User privacy rights

Depending on the law that applies, you may have rights to obtain information, access personal data, correct it, request deletion, restrict or object to processing, receive portable data, or withdraw consent where processing relies on consent. These rights can have conditions and exceptions. You may also have a right to complain to an appropriate privacy regulator. Quiz scores currently provide feedback only; no automated decision with legal or similarly significant effects is implemented. A usable rights-request channel has not yet been established.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: Confirm applicable rights, relevant supervisory authority, response requirements, proportionate identity verification, and a monitored channel through which users can exercise their rights. Do not launch with an unusable request route.

16. Account and data deletion

Accounts are stored in Supabase Authentication. CURIO has no self-service account deletion feature yet. Signing out or clearing browser storage does not delete your account. The operator must establish a monitored deletion-request channel and procedures covering Supabase account records and provider retention before public launch.

17. Children’s privacy

An educational audience may include children. No finalized minimum age, age-verification process, parental-consent mechanism, or child account system exists. Authentication does not establish a parental consent mechanism; children’s account eligibility must be resolved before public launch. Parents and guardians should supervise development testing and avoid providing a child’s personal information.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: Confirm intended age groups and country-specific requirements; decide whether to support children and establish any necessary parental consent, age-appropriate notices, minimization, safeguards, and guardian request process before public launch. No arbitrary age threshold is asserted here.

18. Changes to this policy

This notice will be updated when implementation, providers, purposes, or legal requirements change. The Last Updated date identifies the current version. Before material new processing begins, publish accurate information and provide any required notice or consent choice. Future features described in this draft are not a statement that data is already being collected for them.

19. Contact information

The operator is an individual developing CURIO locally. No public privacy email, business address, or registered company has been confirmed. There is currently no operational privacy request form.

TO CONFIRM BEFORE LAUNCH: Insert the operator/controller’s legal name, required contact address, and monitored privacy email or equivalent channel; add representative or privacy-officer details if legally required.
