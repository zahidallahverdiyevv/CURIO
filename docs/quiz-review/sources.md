# Quiz references

References used to check selected quiz facts and conceptual distinctions. This list covers targeted checks rather than a source for every question.

## History and historical evidence

- [Nobel Prize facts](https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/facts/nobel-prize-facts/): Curie's 1903 physics and 1911 chemistry awards.
- [UN Charter](https://www.un.org/en/about-us/un-charter/): signing on 26 June and entry into force on 24 October 1945.
- [Royal Museums Greenwich — Harrison's timekeepers](https://www.rmg.co.uk/stories/time/harrisons-clocks-longitude-problem): eighteenth-century H4 trials and longitude.
- [Royal Museums Greenwich — Mary Celeste object record](https://www.rmg.co.uk/collections/objects/rmgc-object-40283): the documented abandoned vessel, distinguished from proposed explanations.
- [National Park Service — Roanoke theories](https://www.nps.gov/fora/learn/historyculture/major-theories-of-the-lost-colony.htm): White's return and the CROATOAN inscription.
- [Parks Canada — searching for Franklin](https://www.parks.canada.ca/lhn-nhs/nu/epaveswrecks/culture/histoire-history/suivant-next): Inuit knowledge and the 2014/2016 wreck discoveries.
- [Yale — Voynich manuscript](https://news.yale.edu/2016/10/31/mysterious-voynich-manuscript-reborn-facsimile-edition): parchment dating and the Roger Bacon attribution problem.
- [University of Leicester — Richard III DNA results](https://le.ac.uk/richard-iii/identification/genetics/dna-results): mitochondrial comparisons.
- [English Heritage — understanding Stonehenge](https://www.english-heritage.org.uk/visit/places/stonehenge/history-and-stories/understanding-stonehenge/): solar alignments and limits of interpretation.
- [Greek Ministry of Culture — Antikythera mechanism](https://olympicgames.culture.gov.gr/en/texnologia/1_Mixanismos-Antikithirwn.html): astronomical and calendrical functions.
