Status: review copy of current local-site content; independent editorial review is ongoing. Date: 17 November 2026.

7 quizzes × 15 questions = 105 questions. Each quiz has five Easy, five Medium and five Hard questions. Questions are in the site’s existing English language. Options and answer letters use the same deterministic preparation as the site build.

## Instructions for the reviewer

Review every question independently. Flag factual errors, disputed or time-sensitive claims, multiple defensible answers, weak distractors, inaccurate explanations and difficulty mismatches. Pay particular attention to answer-length clues, grammatical clues, implausible distractors, absolute wording and answer-position patterns. Do not assume the marked answer is correct. Return feedback using quiz slug and question number, explain each problem, cite reliable sources for factual corrections, and suggest corrected text for all four options when needed.

## Mountains & Landforms

### 01 · Easy

Which process mainly creates sand dunes?

**A.** Glaciers carve granite

**B.** Lava hardens underwater

**C.** Coral grows upward

**D.** Wind deposits sand

**Marked answer: D. Wind deposits sand**

**Explanation:** Wind transports loose grains and deposits them where airflow loses carrying power.

Which landform is a broad, elevated area with relatively level ground?

**A.** Valley

**B.** Plateau

**C.** Isthmus

**D.** Peninsula

**Marked answer: B. Plateau**

**Explanation:** A plateau stands above nearby terrain but has a comparatively broad, level surface.

The Andes run mainly along which side of South America?

**A.** Eastern side

**B.** Central plains

**C.** Northern lowlands

**D.** Western side

What is an isthmus?

**A.** A steep river gorge

**B.** A narrow land connection

**C.** A ring-shaped coral reef

**D.** A sheltered ocean inlet

**Marked answer: B. A narrow land connection**

**Explanation:** An isthmus joins larger land areas and separates bodies of water.

### 05 · Easy

Which process wears away rock and carries material elsewhere?

**A.** Crystallization

**B.** Erosion

**C.** Deposition

**D.** Cementation

**Marked answer: B. Erosion**

**Explanation:** Erosion removes and transports material; deposition is its settling elsewhere.

### 06 · Medium

Why are the Himalayas associated with thick continental crust?

**A.** A hotspot lifts a chain above an ocean plate

**B.** A transform fault pulls the crust apart

**C.** A spreading ridge builds new oceanic crust

**D.** Continental collision shortens and thickens crust

**Marked answer: D. Continental collision shortens and thickens crust**

### 07 · Medium

Which process best explains a hanging valley above a larger glacial trough?

**Marked answer: A. A tributary glacier eroded less deeply**

**Explanation:** Smaller tributary glaciers often cut shallower valleys than the main glacier, leaving elevated junctions.

---

### 08 · Medium

What process commonly creates a coastal spit?

**A.** Tidal erosion deepens an offshore trench

**B.** Glacial abrasion exposes a rock ridge

**C.** Longshore drift deposits transported sediment

**D.** River incision leaves an elevated terrace

**Marked answer: C. Longshore drift deposits transported sediment**

**Explanation:** Longshore transport can extend a narrow deposit from the shore into adjacent water.

---

### 09 · Medium

What distinguishes a shield volcano's typical profile from a steep stratovolcano?

**A.** Explosive ash deposits form a deep coastal fjord

**B.** Dissolved limestone leaves a ring of collapse pits

**C.** Fluid lava spreads over broad gentle slopes

**D.** Viscous lava forms narrow overlapping steep domes

**Marked answer: C. Fluid lava spreads over broad gentle slopes**

**Explanation:** Low-viscosity basaltic lava commonly builds broad shield volcanoes; stratovolcanoes have steeper composite structures.

---

### 10 · Medium

Why can a dry region occur on a mountain range's leeward side?

**A.** Descending air warms after windward moisture loss

**B.** Air cools by compression as it descends inland

**C.** Leeward slopes receive the strongest direct ocean evaporation

**D.** Windward clouds transport most rain beneath the ground

**Marked answer: A. Descending air warms after windward moisture loss**

**Explanation:** Windward uplift can remove moisture; subsequent descent warms the air and lowers relative humidity.

---

### 11 · Hard

A trail rises 120 m over a horizontal distance of 1,500 m. What is its average gradient?

**A.** 80%

**B.** 8%

**C.** 18%

**D.** 12.5%

**Marked answer: B. 8%**

**Explanation:** Gradient as a percentage is rise divided by horizontal run, multiplied by 100: 120/1500 × 100 = 8%.

### 12 · Hard

Why can a retreating waterfall leave a gorge behind it?

**A.** Sediment gradually raises the downstream riverbed

**B.** Groundwater dissolves a new underground channel

**C.** Undercutting and collapse move the fall upstream

**D.** A river changes its direction toward the ocean

**Marked answer: C. Undercutting and collapse move the fall upstream**

**Explanation:** Repeated undercutting and collapse can shift the waterfall upstream and extend a gorge downstream.

---

### 13 · Hard

Why do limestone regions often contain caves and sinkholes?

**A.** Carbonated water dissolves calcium carbonate

**B.** Oxidizing water converts limestone into quartz

**C.** Cooling lava contracts beneath the limestone

**Explanation:** Weakly acidic water can dissolve limestone along joints, developing karst drainage and collapse features.

---

### 14 · Hard

**D.** Sand erodes windward and accumulates leeward

**Explanation:** Wind transports grains over the crest; deposition and avalanching on the lee side shift the dune.

### 15 · Hard

Why can removing a sediment-trapping dam alter a downstream delta?

**B.** The catchment boundary moves to the coastline

## Climate & Weather

### 01 · Easy

What is the main difference between weather and climate?

**A.** Timescale of description

**B.** Presence of precipitation

**C.** Direction of prevailing winds

**D.** Amount of cloud cover

**Explanation:** Weather describes short-term conditions; climate describes patterns over much longer periods.

Which instrument measures air pressure?

**A.** Hygrometer

**C.** Barometer

What directly powers most of Earth's weather system?

**A.** Ocean salinity

**B.** Lunar light

**C.** Geothermal heat

**D.** Solar energy

**Marked answer: D. Solar energy**

**Explanation:** Uneven solar heating creates temperature and pressure differences that drive much atmospheric motion.

### 04 · Easy

What does humidity describe?

**A.** Salt content of rain

**B.** Water vapor in air

**C.** Dust concentration aloft

**D.** Wind speed near ground

**Marked answer: B. Water vapor in air**

**Explanation:** Humidity concerns atmospheric water vapor; relative humidity depends on temperature as well.

---

### 05 · Easy

Which cloud type is strongly associated with thunderstorms?

**A.** Cirrostratus

**B.** Stratus

**C.** Altostratus

**D.** Cumulonimbus

**Marked answer: D. Cumulonimbus**

**Explanation:** Cumulonimbus clouds develop strong vertical motion and can produce lightning and heavy rain.

---

### 06 · Medium

Why does rising unsaturated air usually cool?

**A.** Compression removes its water vapor

**B.** Friction converts its heat into altitude

**C.** Expansion does work as pressure falls

**D.** Condensation absorbs heat from the air

**Marked answer: C. Expansion does work as pressure falls**

**Explanation:** As air rises into lower pressure, expansion generally lowers its temperature before condensation begins.

---

### 07 · Medium

What helps moderate seasonal temperatures near many coastlines?

**A.** Water stores and releases heat relatively slowly

**B.** Sea breezes keep daily solar input constant

**C.** Coastal air prevents the ground emitting heat

**D.** Saltwater absorbs the Earth's magnetic energy

**Marked answer: A. Water stores and releases heat relatively slowly**

**Explanation:** Water's heat capacity, mixing and transport moderate temperature changes relative to many inland surfaces.

---

### 08 · Medium

What does a monsoon describe most fundamentally?

**A.** A daily wind caused by a local sea breeze

**B.** A seasonal change in prevailing circulation

**C.** A cyclone crossing a tropical coastline

**D.** A storm exceeding a fixed rainfall threshold

**Marked answer: B. A seasonal change in prevailing circulation**

**Explanation:** A monsoon is a seasonal circulation regime, often associated with marked rainfall changes.

---

### 09 · Medium

Why can the same air mass produce more rain after gaining moisture?

**A.** Its water molecules become chemically heavier

**B.** More vapor is available for condensation

**C.** Its barometric pressure becomes independent of temperature

**D.** The condensation level must rise above all clouds

**Marked answer: B. More vapor is available for condensation**

**Explanation:** When lifting and other conditions permit, additional vapor can support larger precipitation totals.

---

### 10 · Medium

What does a surface albedo of 0.30 mean?

**A.** It emits 30% more sunlight than it receives

**B.** It absorbs 30% of outgoing thermal radiation

**C.** It reflects 30% of incoming solar radiation

**D.** It is 30% warmer than a black surface

**Marked answer: C. It reflects 30% of incoming solar radiation**

**Explanation:** Albedo is the fraction of incoming shortwave radiation reflected by a surface.

---

### 11 · Hard

A city's long-term January mean is 4°C; this January averages 6.5°C. What is the temperature anomaly?

**A.** −2.5°C

**B.** +6.5°C

**C.** +2.5°C

**D.** +10.5°C

**Marked answer: C. +2.5°C**

**Explanation:** An anomaly is the observation minus the reference mean: 6.5 − 4 = 2.5°C.

### 12 · Hard

Why can dense urban surfaces keep a district warmer after sunset?

**C.** Reflected sunlight continues arriving throughout the night

**D.** Tall buildings make the district's solar day longer

**Marked answer: B. Stored heat is released while radiative cooling is limited**

**Explanation:** Heat storage and urban geometry can reduce nighttime cooling; vegetation and evaporation also affect the local energy balance.

### 13 · Hard

Air cools without losing water vapor. Why does its relative humidity rise?

**A.** Cooling removes the dry gases from the air parcel

### 15 · Hard
