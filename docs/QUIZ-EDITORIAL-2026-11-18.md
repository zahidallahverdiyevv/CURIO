# Quiz editorial revision — 18 November 2026

External reviewer feedback has now been received for History, Geography, Science, Philosophy, Art & Culture and Technology. This is a response to that feedback, not an independent approval of the new wording. The 107 revised questions in the four latter categories need another review. Difficulty labels remain editorial estimates, without learner-response calibration.

- Corrected ethical versus psychological egoism, Mill's necessary condition for coercion, utilitarian aggregation including minority interests, Hume's stem, pragmatism's grammatical cue and the modus-tollens explanation. Moved the basic modus-tollens question to Medium and added a multi-step Hard inference.

Removed the cited Rawls, Anansi, artery and stage-layout cross-question clues and the repeated transistor questions across electronics/innovation.

## Answer ordering and review copies

Numeric choices (including percentages, negative numbers, fractions and simple spelled-out numbers) appear in ascending order. Other choices use deterministic shuffled positions, without the former exact 4/4/4/3 count per quiz. No three consecutive answers share one position; no position appears more than seven times in a 15-question quiz. These constraints are editorial safeguards, not a claim that answer patterns are impossible to exploit.

All 42 new pages and their review files were regenerated together. History and Geography wording is unchanged, but their option letters changed. Use **v3 dated 18 November 2026**, and cite quiz slug plus question number. The six original topic quizzes and Daily Six were not regenerated.

The automated length audit reports 81 strictly-longest correct options and 241 strictly-shortest correct options among 630 questions. There are no correct-long outliers at the existing threshold (over 1.65 times median distractor length and more than 14 extra characters). The remaining shortest-answer skew and substantive distractor quality require human judgment; passing the audit does not prove absence of clues.

Original four-category source files and the reproducible edit script are retained under `.curio-backups/editorial-2026-11-18/`. [Latest review copies](quiz-review/README.md) and [targeted references](quiz-review/sources.md) are separate from public website routes.
