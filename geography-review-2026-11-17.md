# CURIO — geography — review draft

Status: authored draft, not yet published. Date: 17 November 2026.

7 quizzes × 15 questions = 105 questions. Each quiz has five Easy, five Medium and five Hard questions. Questions are in the site’s existing English language. Options and answer letters use the same deterministic preparation as the site build.

## Instructions for the reviewer

Review every question independently. Flag factual errors, disputed or time-sensitive claims, multiple defensible answers, weak distractors, inaccurate explanations and difficulty mismatches. Pay particular attention to answer-length clues, grammatical clues, implausible distractors, absolute wording and answer-position patterns. Do not assume the marked answer is correct. Return feedback using quiz slug and question number, explain each problem, cite reliable sources for factual corrections, and suggest corrected text for all four options when needed.

## Climate & Weather

Quiz ID: `climate-weather`

### 01 · Easy

What is the main difference between weather and climate?

**A.** Timescale of description

**B.** Presence of precipitation

**C.** Direction of prevailing winds

**D.** Amount of cloud cover

**Marked answer: A. Timescale of description**

**Explanation:** Weather describes short-term conditions; climate describes patterns over much longer periods.

---

### 02 · Easy

Which instrument measures air pressure?

**A.** Hygrometer

**B.** Rain gauge

**C.** Barometer

**D.** Anemometer

**Marked answer: C. Barometer**

**Explanation:** Barometers measure pressure, while the other instruments measure wind, humidity or precipitation.

---

### 03 · Easy

What directly powers most of Earth's weather system?

**A.** Ocean salinity

**B.** Lunar light

**C.** Geothermal heat

**D.** Solar energy

**Marked answer: D. Solar energy**

**Explanation:** Uneven solar heating creates temperature and pressure differences that drive much atmospheric motion.

---

### 04 · Easy

What does humidity describe?

**A.** Salt content of rain

**B.** Water vapor in air

**C.** Dust concentration aloft

**D.** Wind speed near ground

**Marked answer: B. Water vapor in air**

**Explanation:** Humidity concerns atmospheric water vapor; relative humidity depends on temperature as well.

---

### 05 · Easy

Which cloud type is strongly associated with thunderstorms?

**A.** Cirrostratus

**B.** Stratus

**C.** Altostratus

**D.** Cumulonimbus

**Marked answer: D. Cumulonimbus**

**Explanation:** Cumulonimbus clouds develop strong vertical motion and can produce lightning and heavy rain.

---

### 06 · Medium

Why does air usually cool as it rises in the lower atmosphere?

**A.** It moves farther from sunlight

**B.** It becomes more strongly magnetic

**C.** It expands at lower pressure

**D.** It loses all its water vapor

**Marked answer: C. It expands at lower pressure**

**Explanation:** Rising air expands as external pressure decreases, which tends to cool it.

---

### 07 · Medium

Why do coastal places often have milder temperature swings than inland places?

**A.** Water changes temperature relatively slowly

**B.** Sea air contains no greenhouse gases

**C.** Coasts receive identical sunlight year-round

**D.** Ocean winds prevent nighttime cooling

**Marked answer: A. Water changes temperature relatively slowly**

**Explanation:** Water's heat capacity and mixing moderate nearby temperatures, though local geography also matters.

---

### 08 · Medium

What distinguishes a monsoon from an individual rainstorm?

**A.** A cloud made mainly of ice crystals

**B.** A seasonal shift in wind patterns

**C.** A storm confined to mountain peaks

**D.** A fixed amount of daily rainfall

**Marked answer: B. A seasonal shift in wind patterns**

**Explanation:** Monsoons are seasonal circulation changes, often linked to wet and dry periods.

---

### 09 · Medium

Why can warmer air increase the potential for heavy rainfall?

**A.** It eliminates atmospheric pressure differences

**B.** It can hold more water vapor

**C.** It reduces evaporation from the sea

**D.** It forces every cloud to produce hail

**Marked answer: B. It can hold more water vapor**

**Explanation:** More atmospheric moisture can intensify precipitation when conditions favor condensation and uplift.

---

### 10 · Medium

What is albedo?

**A.** The height of a cloud's base

**B.** The rate of atmospheric rotation

**C.** The fraction of sunlight reflected

**D.** The pressure at sea level

**Marked answer: C. The fraction of sunlight reflected**

**Explanation:** Bright surfaces generally reflect a greater share of incoming sunlight than dark surfaces.

---

### 11 · Hard

Why does one unusually cold week not disprove a warming climate?

**A.** Temperature trends require uniform local weather

**B.** Climate records exclude winter measurements

**C.** Short-term variability can coexist with trends

**D.** Cold spells occur only over the oceans

**Marked answer: C. Short-term variability can coexist with trends**

**Explanation:** Climate trends describe long-term distributions, within which individual cold events can still occur.

### 12 · Hard

An urban district is hotter than nearby countryside at night. Which explanation fits best?

**B.** Built surfaces retain and release heat

**C.** Urban roofs increase Earth's distance from space

Why does relative humidity rise when air cools without gaining moisture?

**A.** Its total pressure must double

**B.** Its oxygen content becomes lower

**C.** Its water vapor becomes heavier

### 14 · Hard

A city floods after paving large areas. What has likely changed?

### 15 · Hard

Why compare climate averages over several decades?

**A.** To exclude unusual years from records

**B.** To make weather forecasts unnecessary

**C.** To remove seasonal differences from Earth
